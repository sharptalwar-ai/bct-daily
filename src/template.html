          How this report is compiled

          Every morning the previous day's report emails from Operations, Housekeeping, Maintenance, Logistics, HR and Accounts, the Flower shop, IT and the Restaurant are read and consolidated into this page. Figures are taken from the reports as written and percentages are recalculated. An item is shown as resolved only when a report says so. "Not seen" means no report reached the Management inbox. Photographs and attachments are not read yet. Personnel matters are kept off this page.
