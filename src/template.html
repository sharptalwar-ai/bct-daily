    How this report is compiled, and what comes next

    Every morning the previous day's report emails from Operations, Housekeeping, Maintenance, Logistics, HR and Accounts, the Flower shop, IT and the Restaurant are read and consolidated into this one page. Figures are taken from the reports as written and percentages are recalculated. An item is shown as resolved only when a report says so. A department shows as "not seen" when no report reached the Management inbox; it may still have been sent elsewhere.

    Photographs and attachments are not read yet. Personnel matters are kept off this page.

    Planned next: department heads update their own items and replies directly on this page, so the register no longer depends on email.
